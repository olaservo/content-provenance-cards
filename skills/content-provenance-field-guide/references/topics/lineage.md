# Topic: lineage

Lineage: how a work or a dataset records what it was made from, such as a parent it was derived from, the parts it is composed of and the inputs to the process that produced it.

- [Agent Skills at-rest signing threads](../cards/agent-skills-signing.md) (proposal, provenance): Seven open discussions in Ideas, none answered by a listed maintainer, no convergence
- [C2PA](../cards/c2pa.md) (organization, provenance): Spec 2.5 published October 2026
- [MCP Financial Services Interest Group](../cards/mcp-fsig.md) (interest-group, provenance, attribution): Charter amended 2026-11-21 to a problem-first forum that routes gaps to Working Groups; data lineage and citation stays in scope
- [OpenLineage](../cards/openlineage.md) (project, provenance): RFC 4484 open (updated 2026-10-07); BearingNode proposed an MCP `_meta` lineage key to the FSIG 2026-10-08
