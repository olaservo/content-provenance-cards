DID-based agent identity, proof of what content the agent is licensed to access, and a link from identity to the telemetry endpoint.

## Topics

- **Agent identity and entitlements**: who the agent is and what content it is licensed to access.

## Compare

- [IETF Web Bot Auth Working Group](ietf-web-bot-auth.md) proves an agent's identity on the wire. The AIMS spec does not mention it (searched 2026-11-15).
- Overlaps the [AAIF Identity and Trust WG](aaif-identity-trust-wg.md). Neither names the other (searched 2026-11-15).
