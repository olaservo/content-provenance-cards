Merchant checkout for agents. [OpenAttribution.org](openattribution.md) has a `content_attribution` extension for it.

## Topics

- **Agent checkout**: letting agents complete merchant checkout.
- **Credit for purchases**: crediting the content that led to a purchase, through the [OpenAttribution.org](openattribution.md) extension.
