Policy language for permissions, prohibitions and duties on content.

## Topics

- **Policy language**: one model for permissions, prohibitions and duties that [Really Simple Licensing](rsl.md), IPTC RightsML ([IPTC](iptc.md)) and [W3C TDM Reservation Protocol Community Group](w3c-tdmrep.md) point at. Its AI vocabulary draft cites the [Creator Assertions Working Group](cawg.md) training and data mining assertion.
- **AI actions**: adding AI-specific actions to the vocabulary (the `ai-vocab` draft).
