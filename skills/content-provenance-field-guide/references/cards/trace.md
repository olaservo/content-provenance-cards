# TRACE

- Id: trace
- Also known as: Trusted Retrieval and Attribution for Content Ecosystems
- Type: project
- Status: Launched 2026-10-06 with funding for three projects
- Website: https://www.countermetrics.org/trace-project/
- Tags: type/project, layer/provenance, layer/attribution, layer/access, medium/text
- Updated: 2026-11-16
- Explorer: https://content-provenance-field-guide.olahungerford.workers.dev/#trace

An initiative of scholarly publishers and standards bodies on how AI systems retrieve, attribute and report their use of scholarly content. It was announced at the STM conference in Frankfurt on 2026-10-06 and brings together STM, [NISO](niso.md), [COUNTER Metrics](counter.md) and Ithaka S+R. It funds three projects and has no site of its own (searched 2026-11-16).

## Topics

- **Counting AI use**: TRACE participants part-fund the [COUNTER Metrics](counter.md) API and JSON Schema work for its AI metrics. COUNTER names nine publishers as contributors, including Elsevier, Springer Nature and Wiley.
- **Provenance at inference time**: [NISO](niso.md) runs a twelve-month pilot on a minimum metadata model for provenance and attribution.
- **How agents reach licensed content**: [The Endpoint Question](https://stm-assoc.org/stm-and-ithaka-sr-launch-research-project-on-the-interface-between-scholarly-content-and-ai/), a three-month Ithaka S+R study announced with STM. It looks at how AI agents discover scholarly content, authenticate through a user's entitlements, receive provenance-bearing information and create a credible record of use. Its scope includes "MCP-style access protocols". Findings are to be published openly.

## Related
