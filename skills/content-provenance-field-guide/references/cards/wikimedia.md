Movement behind Wikipedia and Wikidata. Wikimedia Enterprise sells companies API access to the projects. A WikiCite contributor reviewed an MCP citation proposal.

## Topics

- **Identifiable agents**: knowing which agents are reading the projects.
- **Attribution for reuse**: meeting attribution requirements in agent answers. A WikiCite contributor listed six functions of citation on [SEP-3094 Granular Citations](mcp-sep-3094-citations.md), including provenance chains to primary sources.
- **A seat at the table**: [Open Future](open-future.md) argued in December 2025 that Wikimedia needs one where agent standards are set.

## Compare

- [IETF Web Bot Auth Working Group](ietf-web-bot-auth.md) and [Agent Identity and Manifest Standard](aims.md) are two ways to identify an agent. Wikimedia's post asks for identifiable agents and names no protocol (searched 2026-11-15).
- The seat Open Future argued for is at [Agentic AI Foundation](aaif.md). Neither Wikimedia nor AAIF names the other (searched 2026-11-15).

## In use

- Wikimedia Enterprise partners include Google, Ecosia, Nomic, Pleias, ProRata and Reef Media. Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Mistral AI and Perplexity were announced as partners on 2026-01-15 ([announcement](https://enterprise.wikimedia.com/blog/wikipedia-25-enterprise-partners/)).
