Training via [Shared AI training robots.txt token](aipref-training-token.md) or a page-level `ai-training:disallow`, plus a separate page-level grounding preference (`ai-grounding:disallow`) with no robots.txt form. Replaces `NoArchive` and `NoCache`.

## Topics

- **Inference-time preferences**: letting a page opt out of grounding, the first vendor signal for a use [IETF AI Preferences Working Group](ietf-aipref.md) does not yet cover.

- Microsoft says the preferences do not establish licensing or payment.
