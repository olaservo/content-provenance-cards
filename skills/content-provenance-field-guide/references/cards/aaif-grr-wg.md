## Topics

- **Evidence record shape**: converging record formats for logging and evidence ([issue 9](https://github.com/aaif/wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory/issues/9), closed 2026-11-12 into the interim report).
- **Policy mapping**: mapping regulation onto the projects. The phase 2 input in issue 9 cites EU AI Act Articles 12, 19 and 26(6) ([EU AI Act and GPAI Code of Practice](eu-ai-act-gpai-code.md)). The public repository does not name the GPAI Code of Practice (searched 2026-11-20). The interim report is a Google Doc that needs sign-in.
