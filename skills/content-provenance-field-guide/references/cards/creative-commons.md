Steward of the CC licenses and CC REL machine-readable license metadata. Proposes [CC Signals](cc-signals.md) and filed AIPREF drafts on exclusions and parameters ([IETF AI Preferences Working Group](ietf-aipref.md)). The exclusions draft expired on 2026-10-24.

## Topics

- **AI reuse beyond licensing**: giving stewards terms for AI reuse, since licensing alone cannot address it (September 2026 guidance).
- **Attribution in answers**: honoring the CC attribution requirement when content reaches a model or an agent answer.
- **Source references in answers**: finding early adopters for the CC Signals alpha in 2027. Its SourceConnection component would require AI developers to "provide references to sources when AI systems use outside sources to help answer questions".

## Compare

- [Open Knowledge Foundation](okfn.md), whose MCP server already returns a list of sources, is a candidate early adopter for SourceConnection. No CC page names OKFN's MCP server, and no OKFN page names SourceConnection (searched 2026-11-22).
