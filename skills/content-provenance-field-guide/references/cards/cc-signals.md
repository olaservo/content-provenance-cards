Preference signals for AI reuse with terms attached: Credit, Credit + Direct Contribution, Credit + Ecosystem Contribution, Credit + Open. Layered on [IETF AI Preferences Working Group](ietf-aipref.md) vocabulary.

## Topics

- **Reuse with reciprocity**: letting stewards allow AI reuse with terms attached, for those who do not want a plain opt-out.
- **Checking credit**: knowing whether credit was given.

## Compare

- [Content Telemetry](content-telemetry.md) and [SEP-3094 Granular Citations](mcp-sep-3094-citations.md) are two ways to check whether credit was given. Neither names CC Signals (searched 2026-11-15).
