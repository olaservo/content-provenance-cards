# EU AI Act and GPAI Code of Practice

- Id: eu-ai-act-gpai-code
- Also known as: Article 50
- Type: regulation
- Status: Commission compiling a list of recognized machine-readable opt-out mechanisms
- Website: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/contents-code-gpai
- Tags: type/regulation, layer/access, layer/governance, layer/provenance
- Updated: 2026-11-15
- Explorer: https://content-provenance-field-guide.olahungerford.workers.dev/#eu-ai-act-gpai-code

The copyright chapter of the GPAI Code of Practice has signatories honor robots.txt and make best efforts on other protocols. Article 50 sets transparency duties for AI-generated content.

## Topics

- **Ranking opt-outs**: deciding which mechanisms count. The Commission's [consultation FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/stakeholder-consultation-ai-and-copyright-compliance) lists [W3C TDM Reservation Protocol Community Group](w3c-tdmrep.md) and "C2PA TDM Assertions" ([C2PA](c2pa.md)) among the solutions. [Open Future](open-future.md) and [IPTC](iptc.md) answered the consultation.
- **Transparency for generated content**: Article 50 duties.

## Related

- Google ties [Shared AI training robots.txt token](aipref-training-token.md) support to the Code.

## Compare

- The FAQ does not name [IETF AI Preferences Working Group](ietf-aipref.md), [Really Simple Licensing](rsl.md) or [Creator Assertions Working Group](cawg.md). Open Future's response names the first two, and the assertion the FAQ calls C2PA's is now a CAWG spec (searched 2026-11-15).
- Article 50 is the compliance framing behind the C2PA pitch. Only secondary sources tie the two (searched 2026-11-15).
