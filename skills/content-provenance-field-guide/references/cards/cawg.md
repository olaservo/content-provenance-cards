# Creator Assertions Working Group

- Id: cawg
- Also known as: CAWG
- Type: working-group
- Status: Moved under DIF; consent assertion started as a placeholder draft 2026-07-12
- Website: https://cawg.io
- Part of: [Decentralized Identity Foundation](dif.md)
- Take part: [spec repositories](https://github.com/orgs/decentralized-identity/repositories?q=cawg), via DIF membership
- Tags: type/working-group, layer/provenance, layer/identity, layer/access
- Updated: 2026-11-20
- Explorer: https://content-provenance-field-guide.olahungerford.workers.dev/#cawg

Assertion specs that ride inside [C2PA](c2pa.md) manifests: identity, training and data mining, consent, metadata, endorsement, certificate policy.

## Topics

- **Signer identity**: naming the actor behind a credential, the identity upgrade path for [Agent Skills at-rest signing threads](agent-skills-signing.md).
- **Training and data mining opt-out**: letting an opt-out travel with the asset.
- **Consent**: recording consent as a signed assertion.

## Related

- Its metadata assertion binds metadata from other standards, [IPTC](iptc.md) among them, to the manifest.

## Compare

- The training and data mining assertion is the asset-side counterpart to [IETF AI Preferences Working Group](ietf-aipref.md) and [W3C TDM Reservation Protocol Community Group](w3c-tdmrep.md), which attach an opt-out to a location. The specs do not cite each other (searched 2026-11-15).
