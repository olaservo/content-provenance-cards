Financial-services interest group. Its charter also covers auditability and operational control, which these cards do not cover.

## Topics
- **Regulated data lineage and citation**: tracing financial data through agents. A lineage key for [[openlineage]] was presented on 2026-09-24 ([meeting notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19HwCw7i4k-omABGnRYGGuLdMJNXQc3rs_QRgJxPhQU8/edit)) and filed as [issue 16](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/financial-services-interest-group/issues/16) on 2026-10-08.
- **Charter scope**: the [charter](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/interest-groups/financial-services) lists "Data lineage and citation" and "Data handling" for results that carry usage or redistribution restrictions. It says the group "does not independently sponsor SEPs" and brings unresolved gaps to a Working Group. It meets monthly.

## Related
- [[mcp-sep-3094-citations]] was item 9 on the agenda of the 2026-09-24 meeting. The notes record no discussion of it. "Granular Citations" is listed as a topic for the lineage RFC.
