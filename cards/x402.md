HTTP 402 payments in headers, with schemes `exact`, `upto`, `auth-capture` and `batch-settlement` and transports for HTTP, MCP and A2A. Successor in spirit to L402 (Lightning). Sibling of [[aaif]] under the Linux Foundation, not part of it.

## Topics
- **Machine payment**: paying for a request without accounts or API keys, the rail a licensing term would settle on. The [[rsl]] spec names x402 as a payment protocol.
- **Delegated spending**: authorization for agents spending on someone's behalf, which lives elsewhere ([[ap2]]).

## Related
- Compared with other commerce specs in [[aaif-agentic-commerce-wg]].
- Mark Nottingham's individual draft [draft-nottingham-httpbis-402](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-nottingham-httpbis-402/) defines semantics for the 402 status code. The draft names no payment protocol. Its [IETF 127 slides](https://datatracker.ietf.org/meeting/127/materials/slides-127-httpbis-402-payment-required-00) list x402, h402, L402 and [[mpp]]'s draft-httpauth-payment as current uses of 402. At the [HTTP WG session](https://datatracker.ietf.org/meeting/127/session/httpbis) on 2026-11-20, 15 of 75 participants were interested in adopting it, 3 were not and 18 had no opinion ([minutes](https://datatracker.ietf.org/meeting/127/materials/minutes-127-httpbis-00)).

## Compare
- [[iab-comp]] terms could settle on the same rail. CoMP does not mention x402 (searched 2026-11-15).

## In use
- Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments (generally available since August 2026) pays x402 and [[mpp]] sellers. Since 2026-11-19 an agent can also pay [x402 sellers on AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/enable-ai-agents-to-pay-for-aws-marketplace-x402-products-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-payments/) in USD on its AWS bill, with no wallet. AWS Billing supports only the `exact` scheme at launch.

## History
- 2026-11-20: USDC became the default stablecoin on eight more EVM networks in the TypeScript, Go and Python SDKs ([x402#4337](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/pull/4337)).
- 2026-11-19: AWS added AWS Billing to AgentCore payments for x402 products on AWS Marketplace ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/enable-ai-agents-to-pay-for-aws-marketplace-x402-products-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-payments/)).
- 2026-11-18: Advisory [GHSA-5cgh-j82v-jvrj](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/security/advisories/GHSA-5cgh-j82v-jvrj) (moderate): the SDKs' batch-settlement voucher verifiers accepted signatures the settlement contract cannot recover, so a receiver could hold a voucher whose claim reverts. The advisory lists no patched version. The fixes merged in [x402#4316](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/pull/4316) and [x402#4319](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/pull/4319).
- 2026-10-01: The Linux Foundation appointed Michael Hursta as Executive Director of the x402 Foundation.
- 2026-07-14: x402 Foundation (Linux Foundation) operational.
