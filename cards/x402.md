HTTP 402 payments in headers, with schemes `exact`, `upto`, `auth-capture` and `batch-settlement` and transports for HTTP, MCP and A2A. Successor in spirit to L402 (Lightning). Sibling of [[aaif]] under the Linux Foundation, not part of it.

## Topics
- **Machine payment**: paying for a request without accounts or API keys, the rail a licensing term ([[rsl]], [[iab-comp]]) would settle on.
- **Delegated spending**: authorization for agents spending on someone's behalf, which lives elsewhere ([[ap2]]).
