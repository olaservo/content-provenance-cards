## Topics
- **Policy language**: one model for permissions, prohibitions and duties that [[rsl]], IPTC RightsML ([[iptc]]) and [[w3c-tdmrep]] point at. Its AI vocabulary draft cites the [[cawg]] training and data mining assertion.
- **AI actions**: adding AI-specific actions to the vocabulary (the `ai-vocab` draft).
