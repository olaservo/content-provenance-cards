US standards body for libraries, publishers and the vendors between them. Publishes [[kbart]] and is a partner in [[trace]].

## Topics
- **Provenance metadata for AI retrieval**: a pilot to "develop and test a minimum metadata model that accurately tracks provenance and attribution" when AI systems retrieve scholarly content. It convenes content creators, publishers, repositories, AI tool developers and end-users. NISO's page gives no way to join yet (read 2026-11-16).
- **Naming the version of an article**: [Journal Article Versions](https://www.niso.org/publications/rp-8-2026-jav) (RP-8-2026) defines terms for the versions of a published article and revises the 2008 edition. Steve Smith and Josh Nicholson [point to it](https://scholarlykitchen.sspnet.org/2026/11/16/guest-post-beyond-the-download-what-counts-as-scholarly-content-use-in-the-age-of-ai/) for saying which version an AI usage report refers to.
- **A venue for library standards on AI**: Lisa Janicke Hinchliffe [asks](https://scholarlykitchen.sspnet.org/2026/10/27/mcp-discovery/) for a NISO-style community process to define what a register of publisher MCP connectors should record.

## Related
- The pilot's [launch article](https://scholarlykitchen.sspnet.org/2026/10/07/trace-project-launches-to-advance-ai-interoperability/), by NISO's executive director, names [[c2pa]] content credentials and W3C PROV among existing standards.
- The same article says NISO is beginning conversations with Creative Commons on how a provenance metadata package could fit into [[cc-signals]].
