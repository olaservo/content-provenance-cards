Preference signals for AI reuse with terms attached: Credit, Credit + Direct Contribution, Credit + Ecosystem Contribution, Credit + Open. Layered on [[ietf-aipref]] vocabulary.

## Topics
- **Reuse with reciprocity**: letting stewards allow AI reuse with terms attached, for those who do not want a plain opt-out.
- **Checking credit**: knowing whether credit was given, which links to [[content-telemetry]] and [[mcp-sep-3094-citations]].
