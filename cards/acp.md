Merchant checkout for agents. [[openattribution]] has a `content_attribution` extension for it.

## Topics
- **Agent checkout**: letting agents complete merchant checkout.
- **Credit for purchases**: crediting the content that led to a purchase, through the [[openattribution]] extension.
