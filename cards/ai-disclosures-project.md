Project housed at Code for Science and Society working on the standards and mechanisms for value distribution in the human and AI economy. Prototypes include [[skills-marketplace]], the Open Memory Protocol Consortium and the Open Alpha Registry.

## Topics
- **Value distribution**: the missing mechanisms for attribution and compensation in AI markets.
- **Measuring harm**: ecosystem exploitation in search results and access violations in pre-training data (paper repos).
