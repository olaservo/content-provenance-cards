The copyright chapter of the GPAI Code of Practice has signatories honor robots.txt and make best efforts on other protocols. Article 50 sets transparency duties for AI-generated content.

## Topics
- **Ranking opt-outs**: deciding which mechanisms count. The Commission's [consultation FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/stakeholder-consultation-ai-and-copyright-compliance) lists [[w3c-tdmrep]] and "C2PA TDM Assertions" ([[c2pa]]) among the solutions. [[open-future]] and [[iptc]] answered the consultation.
- **Transparency for generated content**: Article 50 duties.
